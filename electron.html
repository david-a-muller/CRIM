Model limits. Energy loss is continuous (no straggling, no plasmon or core-loss events, no secondary electrons), so the transmitted spectrum width comes only from path-length variation. Screened Rutherford overestimates backscatter for heavy elements at low keV by ~10% compared with Mott cross sections. Bremsstrahlung energy is assumed to escape the sample. Below ~1 keV the Joy–Luo stopping power and the CSDA picture are approximate. Tracking stops at max(50 eV, 0.2% of E₀).
