Model. Same engine as CRIM: amorphous target, ZBL universal potential with magic-formula scattering, ZBL85 electronic stopping with Bragg additivity, TRIM-style free flight, displacement and replacement rules, planar surface binding for sputtering. Ranges typically agree with SRIM within about 10%. Sputter yields are typically within a factor of two of experiment, with Si at low keV underestimated. No channeling, damage build-up or electronic straggling. Statistical scatter between neighbouring points shrinks as the number of ions per point grows; the CSV includes the standard error of Rp.
