CRIM

Claude’s Range of Ions in Materials

Browser-based Monte Carlo tools for charged-particle beams in solids. Everything runs on your own computer inside the browser; nothing is uploaded or installed.

CRIMIon ranges, damage cascades, vacancies and sputtering for 100 eV – 1 MeV ions, in the spirit of SRIM/TRIM. CRIM SweepPlot range, straggle, sputter yield or stopping against beam energy; overlay runs and save PDF or CSV. Electron Beam Monte CarloTrajectories, depth–dose, backscatter and beam broadening for 100 eV – 1 MeV electrons.
Binary-collision approximation, amorphous targets. ZBL85 electronic-stopping coefficients from pyRUMP (MIT License); see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
