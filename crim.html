Model. Amorphous target, binary collisions with the ZBL universal potential (magic formula), ZBL85 electronic stopping with Bragg additivity, no electronic straggling. Free flight follows TRIM: one collision per mean atomic spacing at low energy, longer paths at high energy with a 1 eV minimum energy transfer. Vacancies follow TRIM rules (Ed, Eb, replacement collisions). Sputtering uses a planar surface barrier; sub-threshold recoils within four monolayers of a surface are followed so they can still escape. Expect ranges within about 10% of SRIM. Sputter yields from BCA codes are typically within a factor of two of experiment; Si bombarded at low keV is underestimated. No channeling, no target damage build-up, no compound-bond corrections.
